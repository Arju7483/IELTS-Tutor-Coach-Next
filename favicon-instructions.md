# IELTS Ace Favicon Instructions

## Current Status
- Updated all branding from "IELTS Coach" to "IELTS Ace"
- Changed logo from simple "AI" to a more professional gradient "A" design
- Updated colors to use emerald-blue-purple gradient theme

## Favicon Replacement Needed
Replace `src/app/favicon.ico` with a custom favicon featuring:

### Design Specs:
- **Size**: 32x32px ICO format (minimum), recommend also creating 16x16 and 48x48
- **Design**: Letter "A" in white on gradient background
- **Colors**: 
  - Background: Linear gradient from #10b981 (emerald-500) to #8b5cf6 (purple-500)
  - Text: White (#ffffff)
- **Font**: Bold, modern sans-serif
- **Style**: Rounded corners, professional look

### Alternative Approach:
You can also use online favicon generators like:
1. favicon.io
2. realfavicongenerator.net
3. favicon-generator.org

Just upload a simple square PNG with the "A" design and these tools will generate all required favicon formats.
